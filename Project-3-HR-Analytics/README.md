# Project 3: HR Analytics & Workforce Management Dashboard

## Overview
Comprehensive human resources analytics covering employee performance, retention, compensation, and organizational workforce planning.

## Project Scope
- **Organization**: TechCorp Industries
- **Employee Base**: 2,500+ employees
- **Departments**: 12 major divisions
- **Analysis Period**: FY 2024
- **Data Points**: 50,000+ HR transactions

## Key Metrics
- Total Headcount: 2,547
- Employee Turnover Rate: 12.3%
- Average Tenure: 4.2 years
- Salary Budget: $185M
- Employee Engagement Score: 7.8/10

## Business Insights
1. **Attrition Hotspot**: Engineering dept (18% turnover)
2. **Compensation Gap**: 12% gender pay variance
3. **Engagement Crisis**: Support team (5.2/10 score)
4. **Promotion Rate**: Only 8% annual promotion rate

## Recommendations
1. Implement retention bonus for engineers
2. Address pay equity issues
3. Enhance support team engagement programs
4. Increase promotion velocity to 12%
