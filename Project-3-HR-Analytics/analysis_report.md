## Critical Issues & Challenges

### 1. Engineering Retention Crisis (Priority: CRITICAL)

**Problem Statement**
- Turnover rate: 18% (94 departures)
- Above industry benchmark by 5 percentage points
- Disproportionately losing senior talent
- 2-year tenure representation: 32% of departures

**Business Impact**
- Lost institutional knowledge
- Product development delays
- Hiring costs: $94K × 94 = $8.8M
- Onboarding productivity loss: ~$4.2M
- **Total Cost**: $13M annually

**Root Causes**
1. Compensation gap (avg $8K below market)
2. Limited promotion opportunities (6.9% vs 10% target)
3. Work-life balance concerns (long project cycles)
4. Lack of clear career progression

**Recommended Actions**
1. Conduct market salary analysis and adjust by 10-12%
2. Create specialized career tracks (IC, Leadership, Architecture)
3. Implement retention bonuses for key technical leaders
4. Establish engineering mentorship program

### 2. Customer Support Team Crisis (Priority: HIGH)

**Problem Statement**
- Engagement score: 5.2/10 (lowest in organization)
- Turnover: 16.5% (32 departures)
- Performance rating: 7.3/10
- Low promotion rate: 8.7%

**Business Impact**
- Customer satisfaction declining
- Support quality degradation
- Increased churn risk
- Burnout and attrition cycle

**Root Causes**
1. High workload relative to staffing
2. Low compensation ($68.4K avg, lowest in company)
3. Perceived lack of career growth
4. Emotional labor/burnout
5. Limited recognition programs

**Recommended Actions**
1. Increase support team headcount by 20% (39 FTEs)
2. Implement workload management system
3. Salary increase: +15% ($78.7K target)
4. Create "Support Specialist" career track
5. Enhanced recognition and wellness programs

### 3. Pay Equity Violations (Priority: HIGH)

**Problem Statement**
- Overall gender pay gap: 12.1% (target: <8%)
- Engineering gap: 14.8% (most critical)
- Legal/compliance risk
- Impacts recruitment and retention

**Business Impact**
- Legal exposure: Potential $2-5M litigation
- Recruitment difficulty: Women engineers declining offers
- Retention risk: Aware female employees seeking alternatives
- Reputational damage

**Recommended Actions**
1. Conduct comprehensive pay equity audit
2. Establish remediation budget: $1.2M
3. Adjust salaries for affected employees immediately
4. Implement pay transparency
5. Adjust hiring practices to eliminate baseline gap

### 4. Low Promotion Velocity (Priority: MEDIUM)

**Problem Statement**
- Current rate: 8% vs target: 12%
- Engineering most impacted: 6.9%
- Creates advancement bottleneck
- Reduces employee career satisfaction

**Business Impact**
