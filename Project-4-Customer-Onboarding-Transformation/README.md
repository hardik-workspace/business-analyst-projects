# Customer Onboarding Transformation

## Overview

This project is part of my Business Analysis portfolio and focuses on analysing and improving a customer onboarding process using business analysis, process improvement, and requirements management techniques.

The objective of this case study is to identify process inefficiencies, understand root causes of onboarding delays, and propose practical solutions that improve operational performance and customer experience.

---

## Project Background

A fictional SaaS company was facing challenges in its customer onboarding process including:

- Long onboarding turnaround times
- High dependency on manual activities
- Process bottlenecks
- Customer drop-offs
- Limited operational visibility

The business wanted to improve efficiency, reduce onboarding delays, and create a more consistent onboarding experience for customers.

---

## Business Objectives

### Process Improvement
- Reduce onboarding turnaround time
- Eliminate unnecessary process steps
- Improve operational efficiency

### Customer Experience
- Increase onboarding completion rate
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce customer drop-off during onboarding

### Reporting & Visibility
- Define key performance indicators
- Create dashboard requirements
- Improve data-driven decision making

## Key Challenges Identified

During analysis, the following issues were identified:

1. Multiple manual handoffs
2. Duplicate data entry activities
3. Lack of process ownership
4. Limited onboarding visibility
5. Inconsistent onboarding experience
6. Delayed issue identification

---

## Suggested Improvements

The recommended future-state solution includes:

- Workflow standardization
- Process automation opportunities
- Improved ownership and accountability
- Real-time KPI monitoring
- Better customer communication
- Simplified onboarding journeys
