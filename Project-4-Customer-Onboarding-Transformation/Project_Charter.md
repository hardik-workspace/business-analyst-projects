## 2. BUSINESS PROBLEM STATEMENT

CloudSync SaaS Solutions is experiencing significant operational inefficiency in its customer onboarding process, resulting in delayed time-to-value, increased customer dissatisfaction, and unnecessary operational costs.

### Current State Issues
- **Extended Timeline**: Onboarding process takes 18-24 business days versus industry benchmark of 5-7 days
- **Low Completion Rate**: 76% of customers complete onboarding versus target of 95%
- **Poor Customer Experience**: CSAT score of 6.2/10 significantly below target of 8.5/10
- **High Error Rate**: 12.3% error rate requiring rework versus target of <2%
- **Operational Inefficiency**: 68% manual processes with 8% rework rate
- **System Fragmentation**: Four disconnected systems requiring manual data re-entry
- **Visibility Gap**: Limited real-time tracking and reporting of onboarding progress
- **Resource Drain**: Process requires 12 FTEs with limited scalability

### Business Impact
- **Financial**: $850K annual revenue loss from customer churn
- **Customer Relations**: 19% of new customers rate experience as poor (score <5)
- **Operational**: 40% of team time spent on rework and redundant tasks
- **Strategic**: Scalability constraint limiting revenue growth

## 4. PROJECT SCOPE

### In Scope
1. **Process Redesign**
   - Current state process mapping (As-Is)
   - Future state process design (To-Be)
   - Root cause analysis of inefficiencies
   - Optimization opportunities identification

2. **Technology Implementation**
   - Customer Onboarding Platform selection and deployment
   - Integration with existing systems (CRM, Billing, Product)
   - Workflow automation configuration
   - Reporting and analytics implementation

3. **Change Management**
   - Stakeholder communication and engagement
   - Staff training and capability development
   - Process documentation and guidelines
   - Change adoption monitoring

4. **Quality Assurance**
   - Process design review and validation
   - System integration testing
   - User acceptance testing (UAT)
   - Post-implementation support

5. **Governance**
   - Steering Committee oversight
   - Project status reporting
   - Risk management and mitigation
   - Issue resolution and escalation

### Out of Scope
- Customer billing system modifications (separate initiative)
- Product feature enhancements beyond platform integration
- Marketing collateral redesign
- Organizational restructuring beyond Onboarding team
- Customer data migration from legacy systems
